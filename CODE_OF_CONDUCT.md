# Code of conduct

ByteScape is made by volunteers for anyone who wants to understand their disk. Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull
requests, discussions or anywhere else in the project — is expected to make that a good experience for others.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new or who write in a language that is not their first.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Disagree with reasons, and accept that a decision may go the other way.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding.
- Help others find their way: point to the right place, explain how, share what you learned.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, trolling, or repeatedly derailing a discussion.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules, and may block people from the project temporarily or
for good. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security** tab
("Report a vulnerability" reaches them privately), or open an issue if it can be discussed in public.
