What it does

Treemap of any drive or folder

Drawn while it scans. Double-click to zoom in, Backspace to go back; colours by file type.

"Is it safe to delete?"

Known caches and temp folders are marked and explained, with what deleting them does and how to clean them properly.

Safe cleanup

Delete to the Recycle Bin. Windows and program folders are protected; large, temporary and old installation files are listed for you.

Programs on disk

Every installed program with everything it uses: install folder, app data, caches and Store app data.

Games and developer leftovers

Steam, Epic Games and GOG games you no longer play; old projects' node_modules, bin/obj and other rebuildable folders.

What changed and what comes next

Compare with an earlier scan, see the drive's history, and when it will be full at the current pace.

Duplicates and folder comparison

The same content found by size and SHA-256; one copy always stays. Compare two folders, optionally byte by byte.

Fast

Parallel scanning, quick rescans from the NTFS change journal, pause and continue later. Hard links are counted once.

For everyone

Keyboard, screen reader and high contrast support. English, Turkish, Russian, Chinese, French, Spanish and Portuguese.

Pictures

The pictures show a made-up drive.
